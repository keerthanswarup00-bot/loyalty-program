What Innondu is

Innondu is software that runs a digital loyalty card for a shop. Customers collect stamps by tapping an NFC tag or scanning a QR code, and redeem rewards at the counter. Innondu is provided by .

What it is not

Innondu is not a billing, payments or point-of-sale system. It does not touch your billing, and it is meant to run alongside it. It is not a replacement for keeping your own records.
