About

One more visit is what a shop runs on.

Innondu is a small rewards web app for neighbourhood shops. It does one job: give people a reason to come back.

The name

ಇನ್ನೊಂದು means “one more”.

One more stamp. One more coffee. One more visit. That is the whole idea.

Your brand, not ours
