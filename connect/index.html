You edit it yourself

Change it from your phone, in a minute.

You sign in with the same account as your owner dashboard, on your own page. Edit your details, add or hide items, drop in photos, then tap “View my page”. The link stays the same, so anything you have already printed keeps working.

  1. Edit

    Your details, your list, your photos — from a phone or a laptop.

  2. Preview

    A live preview sits next to the form on a bigger screen.

  3. Share

    Copy the link, send it on WhatsApp, or download the QR code.

Printed and physical

One link that also lives on a counter.

The page’s address is the thing to print or write. The QR code opens it with a scan; an NFC keychain opens it with a tap. Both point at the same page, so you only set it up once.

  1. QR

    Download it from your editor and put it on the counter, the table or the receipt.

  2. NFC keychain

    Write the same link to a tag with a free app — a tap opens the page.

  3. The loyalty card

    Customers tap the stamp tag from the card button on your page.

Who it is for

Built around cafes, and useful for any shop.

The Connect page is configured per shop type — a cafe gets a menu, a salon gets a price list, a repair shop gets its services. We launch it fully for cafes first; other types use the same page with their own labels and sample content, and we add more as they are asked for.
