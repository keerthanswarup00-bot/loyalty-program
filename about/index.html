About

One more visit is
what a shop runs on.

Innondu is a small rewards web app for neighbourhood shops. It does one job: give people a reason to come back.

How it started

We went looking for a simple rewards app. It didn't exist.

The search

We looked for an app that did only rewards. Every option came bundled with billing, stock and a hundred things a shop never asked for.

The tangle

Shops already run a professional billing system. A second system that tries to replace it, or fights with it, is the last thing they need at the counter.

The idea

Build the opposite. Something that stays out of billing, wears the shop's own brand, and can be set up without a developer. Get digital stamp cards for your shop.

Innondu

At the counter the owner does one thing: redeem. Everything else is the customer tapping in, collecting stamps and walking back through the door.
