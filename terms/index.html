Terms of use.

The plain terms for using Innondu to run a loyalty card at your shop.

What Innondu is

Innondu is software that runs a digital loyalty card for a shop. Customers collect stamps by tapping an NFC tag or scanning a QR code, and redeem rewards at the counter. Innondu is provided by .

What it is not

Innondu is not a billing, payments or point-of-sale system. It does not touch your billing, and it is meant to run alongside it. It is not a replacement for keeping your own records.

Your shop account

You are responsible for your login, for the messages you send through Innondu, and for having a lawful reason to store your customers’ names and phone numbers. Innondu stores them so the card can work; you are the shop that owns the list.

Customer data and consent

Your customers’ data belongs to your shop. When you collect a customer’s phone number or email, you must have the consent needed to do so (see our privacy notice, which forms part of these terms). You can export the data as a spreadsheet at any time, and ask us to delete it.

Fees

The plans and prices are on the pricing section of the home page. Longer plans are paid upfront. Price changes are announced before they apply.

Cancelling and refunds

Try it first. Every shop gets a free one-month pilot with nothing to pay.

Monthly plan. Cancel any time. The plan stops at the end of the month you have already paid for. We do not refund a month that has started.

1 Year and 2 Years plans. You pay upfront and get a lower price for it. If you leave early, we refund the months you have not used. The months you did use are charged at the Monthly price (  a month), not the discounted one, and a part month counts as a full month. This way the discount is only kept by shops that stay, and you never pay more than if you had chosen Monthly.

If we stop. If we shut the service down, or it is not working for a long time and we cannot fix it, we refund your unused months at the price you paid.

How. Ask us on WhatsApp or through the contact form. We refund to the way you paid, within 7 working days. The NFC stamp and poster are yours to keep. Your customer data can be exported before you leave and is deleted if you ask.

Availability

We work to keep Innondu running, but it is provided “as is” without a guarantee of uninterrupted service. Keep your own export of customer data as a backup.
