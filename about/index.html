What it is

Rewards, customers and a page of your own.

Innondu does

  • Rewards. A digital stamp card and a daily scratch card.
  • Customer management. See who comes, who went quiet, and win them back on WhatsApp.
  • Your own page. Directions, call, WhatsApp and what you sell, in one link.

Innondu leaves alone

  • Your billing. It runs beside your billing software, never instead of it.
  • A new app. Your customers use it in their browser.
  • Your business. No takeover of your setup, your staff or your process.
Trust

What you are trusting us with.

Plain language, no legal fog. If any of this is not right for your shop, do not use Innondu.

An NFC stamp on your counter

A customer taps the tag or scans the poster. It only adds a stamp to their card. Nothing is installed on their phone, and nothing changes in how you bill.

Your customers’ phone numbers

To run a card we store a customer’s name, phone number, stamps and rewards. That is the minimum needed to give a stamp and find the person at your counter.

Who owns the data

The shop owns its customer list. Innondu is a custodian: we run the software and keep it secure. We do not sell it, and we never use it to market anything to your customers.

Leaving, with your data

You can stop any time. Export every customer as a spreadsheet from your dashboard, and ask us to delete the shop’s data. Both are free.
