Digital stamp cards, a daily scratch card and offers for your café. On your customers’ phones. Nothing to install, and nothing to change in your billing.

Why a paper card is not enough for a café

A paper punch card gets lost in a wallet, forgotten at home or stamped one too many times. It also tells you nothing: you cannot see who your regulars are, how often they come, or who stopped showing up.

Innondu moves the stamp card to your customers' phones. They collect stamps by tapping, you keep serving, and the reward happens on its own once the card is full.

How Innondu works in a café

  1. Put the NFC stamp by the till. We give you an NFC stamp and a QR poster for phones without NFC, free.
  2. Regulars tap to collect. A new customer joins with a name and phone number. After that, every visit is one tap and one stamp. A waiting time between stamps, which you set, stops repeat stamping.
  3. Redeem in seconds. When the card is full, the customer gets a 6-character code. Your barista types it into the owner dashboard, confirms, and hands over the free coffee. Each code works once.

What your regulars get

A stamp card on their phone

It runs in the phone's browser. There is nothing to install, and one tap adds it to the home screen. It shows your café's name, colour and logo, not ours.

A daily scratch card

A small reason to open the card every day, with a sample prize such as “10% off your next visit”.

Welcome, surprise and birthday offers

A welcome offer when they join, a surprise offer at a stamp number you pick, and a birthday offer that is valid only on the birthday. Every offer has its own code and an expiry you control.

What you get as the owner

  • Customers: who comes, how often, and who has gone quiet.
  • Reach out: a ready WhatsApp message, one tap, for birthdays and for regulars you have not seen in a while.
  • Menu: add items and photos and your customers see them.
  • Redeem: type the code, confirm, done.
  • Offers: your rules and your rewards, changed any time, with no reprint.

No new POS, no app, no developer

Cafés already run a billing system that works. Innondu does not replace it and does not connect to it. At the counter your staff do one thing: redeem a code. Everything else is the customer tapping in and collecting stamps.

You choose how many stamps a card needs (anywhere from 2 to 20), what the reward is, how long a card stays valid, and whether new members get a free first stamp. You can change all of this later without a developer.

Café owners’ questions

Will my regulars need to download a loyalty app?

No. The card runs in the phone browser and can be added to the home screen with one tap. Nothing is installed from an app store.

Can I choose how many stamps a card needs and what the reward is?

Yes. You can set anything from 2 to 20 stamps and write the reward in your own words, for example "Free coffee of your choice". You can change it later.

How do I stop customers collecting stamps again and again?

You set a waiting time between stamps, in minutes or hours. If the stamp link ever leaks, you can generate a new one and rewrite your NFC tag.

Does it work with my existing billing or POS?

Innondu does not connect to billing and does not replace it. Your staff only redeem a code, so there is nothing to integrate.

What happens to a card that is never finished?

You choose how long a card lasts from the first stamp, or set it never to expire. A full card never expires, so a customer can always claim a reward they have earned.

Can I run a welcome or birthday offer for my café?

Yes. You can give a welcome offer when someone joins, a surprise offer at a stamp number you pick, and a birthday offer that is valid only on the birthday. Each has its own code and expiry.
