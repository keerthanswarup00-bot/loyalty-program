Privacy notice.

What Innondu stores, who owns it, and how to take it with you or have it deleted.

Who we are

What we store

For a shop’s customers, the loyalty card needs a name, a phone number, and the stamps, rewards and offers that belong to that card. For the shop owner, we store the email and password used to sign in and the brand and offer settings.

We do not collect anything else: no contact list, no location, no payment details.

Why we hold it

Only to run the card. A stamp has to be counted, a reward has to be found at the counter, and a message the owner chooses to send has to reach the right person. Nothing is used to build a profile or to advertise Innondu or anyone else to your customers.

Who owns it

The shop owns its customer list. Innondu is a custodian: we run the software and keep it secure, but the data about a shop’s customers belongs to that shop.

Who can see it

The shop’s own login can see its customers. Innondu runs on hosted infrastructure (Supabase) that stores the data on our behalf, under access rules that keep each shop apart. We do not sell the data, and we do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

For as long as the shop uses Innondu. When a shop asks to leave, the data is exported and then deleted.

Your choices

  • Customers: ask the shop to see or delete your card. The shop can do both from its dashboard.
  • Shops: export every customer as a spreadsheet, and ask us to delete the shop’s data. Both are free.
