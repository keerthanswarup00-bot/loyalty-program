Privacy notice.

What Innondu stores, why, who can see it, and how to take it with you or have it deleted.

Who we are

Innondu is run by (). For any privacy question, write to  .

What we collect

When a shop sets up Innondu, we collect the shop name, owner email address, and shop details (area/city) needed to run the loyalty card. When customers join a shop’s card, we collect their name and phone number. As customers use the card, we store visit/stamp history and reward history. We do not collect sensitive personal data beyond what is necessary.

Why we collect it

To operate the loyalty card: track stamps and rewards, help the shop identify a customer at the counter, and communicate with the shop when needed. We only collect and process data for the purpose stated.

Who sees it

The shop owner (and any staff the owner authorises) sees their own shop’s customer names, phone numbers, and visit/stamp history from the owner dashboard. Innondu (operated by ) has access to the data needed to provide and maintain the service (hosting). We do not sell or rent customer data.

Third parties

We use hosting (Supabase) to store data securely. Clicking WhatsApp links opens WhatsApp (a third-party service); those links are generated with a prefilled message and the shop’s configured number. We do not add tracking pixels to WhatsApp links beyond what is necessary to construct the link. No other third-party advertising or analytics services are used by default.

Cookies and storage

The public site uses minimal cookies/storage only as required for basic functionality (e.g. the app keeps the signed-in shop context locally). Where used, it is strictly functional and not for cross-site tracking.

How long we keep it

We keep customer data for as long as the shop is actively using Innondu. On request, we will delete a shop’s data or export it (see below). Backups are retained for a short period only to help restore from accidental loss.

Your rights (export, deletion and consent)

Under applicable law (including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you may ask for access, export or erasure of data. For shops, export and deletion are available from the dashboard. For customers, you can ask the shop to export or delete your card. For direct requests, contact  .

Consent: by providing your phone number or email through our forms, you consent to our collecting and processing that information for the purpose described here. You can withdraw consent by requesting deletion.

Children

Innondu is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Grievances

If you have a privacy concern, contact  . We will acknowledge and address it in line with our obligations. You can also reach directly through the contact page.
