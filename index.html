The problem

Your regulars keep the lights on, and most shops can’t see them.

The paper card is in another wallet.

Customers forget it, lose it, or never pick it up. A stamp that never gets collected is a visit that never gets rewarded.

You don’t know who comes back.

No names, no numbers, no way to wish someone a happy birthday or tell them an offer is about to end.

Big loyalty tools want your whole till.

Many ask you to change your billing, your POS or your menu setup before a single customer gets a stamp.

What Innondu is

A rewards add-on, not another system to run your shop.

Your billing, GST and stock stay exactly where they are. Innondu is a web page your customers open and a dashboard you open. Nothing to install at the counter and no hardware to wire in.

Never touches

  • Billing and invoices
  • Your POS
  • Payments and GST
  • Inventory and stock
  • Your staff workflow

Only handles

  • Stamps and rewards
  • Offers and coupons
  • Daily scratch cards
  • Your customer list
  • Reaching out to regulars

A visit, start to finish

Four taps from the counter to the reward.

No scanning apps and no cards to carry. The customer joins once with a phone number and a password, then it stays on their phone.

  1. STEP 1

    Tap or scan

    The customer taps their phone on the NFC stamp or scans the QR on the poster. The card opens in their browser.

  2. STEP 2

    The stamp lands

    One stamp per visit. A cooldown you set stops two stamps in the same minute.

  3. STEP 3

    Scratch today’s card

    A fresh scratch card every day gives people a small reason to open the card again tomorrow.

  4. STEP 4

    Claim at the counter

    A full card turns into a 6-character code. Staff type it in, check it, confirm it. Used codes stay used.

Everything the customer gets

Simple for them, so they actually use it.

A branded card

Your name, logo, colour, tagline and links to Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp and your website.

Daily scratch card

Scratch it on the phone, reveal today’s prize. You choose what can be won.

Stamps by tap or scan

NFC stamp or QR code, whichever the customer prefers. Your stamp image can replace the default tick.

Welcome, birthday and surprise offers

A gift when they join, a coupon valid only on their birthday, and a surprise on the stamp numbers you pick.

Coupon codes staff can check

Every offer and reward carries its own code, so nothing can be used twice or by the wrong person.

Clear dates, no surprises

Customers see when an offer or card ends, with a heads-up in the last few days.

On their home screen

One tap to add the card to the phone. It is a web app, so there is nothing to download.

Phone number and password

No email and no one-time codes to wait for at the counter.

The owner dashboard

Easy to find your way around, easy to keep track.

One screen for the day, a few tabs for the rest. Built for the owner or whoever is at the counter, with no training needed.

Know your customers

Every member in one searchable list: stamps, visits, rewards, last visit and card end date. Export it any time.

Reach out in one tap

Birthdays this week, offers about to end and cards about to run out each come with a ready-made WhatsApp message.

Redeem rewards

Type the customer’s code, check it, confirm it. Every redemption is logged and searchable.

Manage your menu

Keep your menu up to date on the same dashboard your customers already use.

Settings you control

Custom offers, stamps needed, cooldown, how long a card lasts, brand colour, logo and stamp image. Changes apply to everyone straight away.
